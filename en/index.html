A new app for studying and for remembering. It uses the oldest memory technique there is, the memory palace, with your phone camera: every fact stays attached to a real spot in your home.

Palacio

Memorise by walking around your home.

You measure a room once with the camera and, from then on, you can stick whatever you want to remember in its corners: study cards, where things are, or voice messages. When you come back, the camera guides you to each spot and shows you what you left there. The brain remembers places far better than lists.

  • Create cards from a photo of the book, by dictating or by typing
  • Review while walking: go to the spot, aim and answer
  • Spaced repetition: each card comes back just before you forget it
  • “Where is…?”: search by voice and an arrow leads you to the spot
  • Voice messages from the family that play in their spot
  • Everything stays on the phone, no internet, no accounts · 4 languages

Two ways to use it

For studying

Exams, civil-service tests or languages. Take a photo of the book page and Palacio creates the cards, hiding the key fact: a date, a number or the important word. Then you place them around the house, the constitution on the mirror and the articles on the door, and review as you walk.

For remembering

Also designed for older people or people with memory problems, and for those who care for them. Save where the keys, the glasses or the medicines are, with large text and few buttons. A son or daughter can record a message that plays when the camera points at that spot: “Dad, your evening pills are here.”

Photos, cards and messages stay only on your phone. Requires an Android phone that supports Google augmented reality (ARCore).

Five things you can make

Lithophanes

A photo that appears when a light shines behind it. Choose the shape, size, thickness and frame, and preview it in 3D unlit and lit.

Lamps

Draw the silhouette with your finger or start from a vase, a globe or a bell. Add twist, ribs, waves and holes, and choose the base for your bulb or LED.

Relief figures

From a photo with a plain background: a standing figure with its base, a keychain or a medallion. Litho3D removes the background and adds relief based on brightness.

3D monuments

A front photo of the Eiffel Tower, a lighthouse or a building, with the sky behind it. Litho3D cuts out the silhouette by itself and turns it into a 3D model with a base. Add a side photo for cathedrals and long buildings too.

Lamp parts

Everything you need to assemble the lamp: a base with a cable slot, the tube split into screw-together sections that fit your printer, a head for a standard bulb holder and a canopy to hang it from the ceiling. The electrical parts come from a shop: the plastic never touches the power.

Works with any filament printer: Anycubic, Creality, Bambu Lab, Prusa, Elegoo, Sovol… The STL file opens in Cura, Anycubic Slicer, Bambu Studio, PrusaSlicer or Orca.
